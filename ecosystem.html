01 — The four primitives

Everything here is one of four things, and each is the consequence of an architecture choice.

Not features bolted on. Each one exists because the base layer made it possible, and each one is why the base layer is worth running. Read the mechanism, not the promise — section 02 is where the yield actually gets justified.

Buy & Sell

Fiat in, self-custody out

Card or bank, direct to your own address. No exchange account, no withdrawal queue, no thirty-day hold. 41 regions, 0.9% all-in.

Swap

Intent router, 0.04% flat

You sign an intent. Solvers bid to fill it and carry the slippage. There is no AMM curve to be sandwiched against.

Stake

Bond to a sphere, 35% APY

Stake follows the sphere you use and secures its proposer set. Rewards are fee revenue plus surplus, not inflation. Seven-day unbond, 27% structural floor.

Stay on chain

Withdrawing is the expensive move

Every primitive here compounds with the others. Pulling to a CEX mid-epoch breaks all three and costs you the bond and the seat.

02 — The number you don't believe

Thirty-five per cent a year compounds at 2.53% a month. Here is where it comes from.

At face value that is four times a good lending market, so it deserves to be taken apart rather than asserted. It is four sources stacked, and only the last one is a subsidy. The other three are revenue a normal venue collects and keeps as margin.

2.1 Strip the subsidy out and the structural base is 27.0% APY — 2.01% a month, 1.27× over twelve months. That is the number to underwrite. Plan around 35%, but do not assume 35% survives epoch 15.

2.2 Subsidy drawdown publishes weekly against the funding address. When it hits zero the rate steps to the structural column and the changelog records it before the step, not after.

2.3 Nothing here is paid in newly minted SPHERE. Inflation is 2 million coins a year on a 100 million float and none of it routes to stakers. If fee revenue halves, the rate halves — that is the honest exposure.

2.4 Yield accrues per block on bonded principal, compounds at claim, and is claimable without unbonding. Compounding at 35% for twelve months is a 1.35× multiple; at 27% it is 1.27×.

2.5 Every structural line scales with volume and nothing else. At a quarter of today's volume the structural rate is 6.8% APY — still above a CEX, well below the headline. The full three-row table and the tripwire that would falsify it are at stake.html §3.

What you are actually paying for itA seven-day unbonding queue, and slashing that reaches bonded principal capped at 5% an epoch. Your principal is illiquid for a week after you ask and the queue is not skippable. Free money with no lock and no downside is the thing that smells — this has both, and that is the whole reason the rate can exist.

04 — Buy & Sell

Fiat to self-custody, and back, without an account in the middle.

An exchange account is a place your balance goes to sit. Here it never sits anywhere but your own address — the ramp is a pipe, not a vault, and there is no withdrawal queue because there is nothing to withdraw from.

1

Choose amount and region

41 regions, 0.9% all-in, the fee shown before you commit rather than after.

2

Partner KYC, once

Regulated partner carries the check. One time, then never again. Documents stay with them.

3

Fund by card or bank

Card clears in under two minutes. Bank is slower and cheaper. Both land on chain.

4

Delivered to your address

Straight to self-custody. No intermediate balance, no hold period, nothing to withdraw later.

Selling is the same path reversed and settles at the same 0.9%. There is no separate sell spread, which is where most ramps hide the margin.

05 — Swap

No curve, so nothing to sandwich.

An AMM prices along a curve that moves as you trade it, and a searcher who sees your transaction can move the curve first. An intent has no curve to move. You state the outcome you will accept, solvers compete to deliver it, and the winner carries the slippage risk on their own book.

Mechanism

Sealed bid auction

Bids close at the block boundary. Solvers see the intent, not each other's bids. The best execution wins, not the fastest sender.

Surplus

Above-floor bids return to you

If the floor is 1,000 USDT and the winner bids 1,002, you get 1,002. The venue takes 0.04% and nothing else.

Failure

Unfilled costs nothing

No fill inside your limit and the intent expires unsigned against your balance. No reverted gas, no stuck approval.

A solver that fails to fill repeatedly loses auction priority for the next epoch. Execution quality is the only thing they can compete on — there is no rebate to buy rank with.

The same surplus pays the bondSurplus is not only returned to the swapper. It is the largest single line in the staking decomposition at stake.html §2 — the staker is the counterparty who guarantees your fill, and the LP's old cut is now theirs. Same mechanism, two sides.
06 — Stake

Your bond secures the sphere you actually use.

Execution shards into named spheres with independent proposer sets. Stake follows the sphere, so bonding to the one you transact on puts your capital behind the blocks your own orders land in. You are not underwriting a stranger's chain.

Accrual

Per block, claimable without unbonding

Yield accrues every block and compounds at claim. Claiming never touches principal and never starts the queue.

Exit

Seven days, not skippable

Ask and the principal queues for seven days. The lock is the price of the rate — there is no fast lane.

Slashing

Bounded, published

Double-sign costs 1% of bond. Downtime costs pro-rata. Maximum in a single epoch is capped at 5%.

Selection

Sphere choice is yours

Rebond between spheres without unbonding. Move off a sphere with degrading uptime in one signature. Rates run 23.8% to 35.0% APY by sphere.

The 35% headline is the best sponsored sphere and sponsor admission is the sponsor's call, not ours. The open-sphere range is 23.8–27.2% APY and needs nobody's permission.

07 — Security

"Military grade" is an adjective. These are the standards it means.

Anyone can write the phrase. What matters is whether the claim cashes out into a named standard with a number attached, and whether the weakest link is a human. Ours is key custody, so key custody is where the design is heaviest.

Keys

FIPS 140-3 Level 3 HSM

Signing keys never exist in plaintext outside the module, and never in browser memory. Extraction requires physical tamper, which zeroises.

FIPS 140-3 L3 · tamper-responsive · non-exportable
Encryption

AES-256-GCM at rest, TLS 1.3 in flight

GCM not CBC, so tampering fails closed rather than decrypting to garbage. Keys rotate every epoch and old material is destroyed.

CNSS-approved for TOP SECRET · FIPS 197 · RFC 8446
Authority

4-of-7, three jurisdictions

Seven signers, HSM-backed, no signer holding two shares. No operator and no pair can move funds.

Geographic split · no shared custodian

Never infinite. Capped at the exact amount, dead in 30 days, and the same allowance serves every later venue.

08 — Why stay on chain

Pulling to an exchange breaks all three primitives at once.

The instinct after a good trade is to move the profit somewhere "safe". For a balance doing nothing, an exchange is fine. For a balance doing this, it is a downgrade.

The bond stops earning and starts queueing

Unbonding is seven days. A round trip to a CEX and back is longer than that once withdrawal windows are counted, and the yield does not accrue in the queue.

The seat is bound to the address

A claim leaf is the address that was scored. Move the balance and the leaf stays put. See §10.3.

Execution quality gets worse, not better

Exchange order books are thin in the pairs that matter here and you pay a spread on both sides. The router has no spread to pay.

You re-enter the queue at the back

Coming back means a fresh bond, a fresh seven-day clock, and a worse position in the sphere's reward weighting.

10 — Before you ask

The objections, in the order they arrive

"35% is a farm number"

27% is structural and 8% is subsidy, and we tell you which is which. Underwrite the 27%. None of it is printed — §2.3. Section 02 itemises all four sources.

"So what happens at low volume"

At a quarter of today's volume the structural rate is 6.8% APY. Still above a CEX. The full table and the falsifying tripwire are at stake.html §3.

"Why is the surplus line bigger than the fee line"

Because the staker replaced the LP. The venue cut is 0.04% instead of 0.30% precisely so the surplus can go to bond instead of to a pool. stake.html §2.1.

"What if the subsidy runs out"

The rate steps to the structural column and the page announces it before the step, not after. It decays linearly over eight epochs so the decline is predictable from day one.

"Seven days is too long to wait"

Then do not bond the whole balance. Nothing here forces a lock, and unbonded SPHERE still swaps at 0.04%.

"What does connecting let through"

Nothing on connect itself. Later, a Permit2 allowance capped at the exact amount and dead in thirty days. Full walkthrough.

"Can I use a hardware wallet"

Yes, throughout, and it is the recommended setup. Nothing requires a hot key.

"Is any of this available to me"

Buy & Sell runs in 41 regions and staking is open everywhere. The seat program excludes 22, listed in full at §11. The venue works in restricted regions; only the seat does not.
