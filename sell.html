01 — Flow

What leaves, what arrives, and when

The token leg settles in seconds; the fiat leg is the slow part and it is the partner's, not yours to chase. Both legs are timestamped and both are on the receipt.

The token leg prices through the intent router at 0.04% and competing solvers fill it. No spread is baked into the quote — the router price prints next to the fiat figure and the fiat fee is its own line. A quote you cannot decompose is a quote you cannot check.

03 — Terms worth reading

Where off-ramps usually go wrong

Cap

Amount, not balance

The signature permits the figure you named for that order and nothing else. Your remaining SPHERE is untouched and out of reach of the partner.

Expiry

Ninety seconds, then dead

Unsigned after the window? The order re-quotes. Signed and unfilled? It expires and the allowance goes with it — nothing left to revoke later.

Names

Payout must match KYC

Third-party bank accounts are refused by most rails. Use the account in your own name — a joint account is fine, a friend's is not.

Tax

Reports you can hand over

Per-order CSV with fiat in, fiat out and timestamps. No reconstruction from block explorers, no estimating the year.

If a payout is held, your tokens are not with the partnerEvery off-ramp holds some orders and most hold them silently. The four reasons here are a name mismatch, source of funds, a bank rejecting the credit, and an abandoned order. In all four the token leg sits in the order escrow, visible at the order address, and returns to you automatically if the fiat leg fails — nothing is sold until payout is confirmed. Past five business days the order auto-cancels and the escrow releases without waiting on the partner.
