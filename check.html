    01 — What the check reads

    Five clauses, and only two of them are a surprise.

    Age, transaction count, cluster, attestation, region. The first two are read straight off public state. The rest confirm at the snapshot, and the page tells you which is which rather than pretending all five are live.

    Read now

    Age and activity

    First outbound transaction and settled count, excluding self-transfers. Deterministic, no discretion, no committee.

    Confirmed later

    Cluster, attestation, region

    Funding-graph grouping at the snapshot. Attestation caps you at T3 without it — it does not exclude you.

    02 — What the figure is

    05 — Objections

    The four that arrive on this page

    "Does checking connect my wallet?"
    No. You paste an address and read public state. Connecting happens only when you press Continue, and the first signature is after that.
    "I failed a clause. Am I out?"
    Only if it is one of the hard four. Attestation caps you at T3 rather than excluding you, and a failed clause tells you whether it is still fixable before the snapshot. Continue is live either way — a blocked result is a fix list, not a dead end.
    "Do I need to buy anything?"
    No. One approve is about nine cents of gas and the signature is free. If you hold anything on the chain you already have enough. Buying USDT is optional.
    "Can I check a different wallet?"
    As many as you like, in a row. Nothing binds until a claim is signed, and the seat binds to the address that signed it.
