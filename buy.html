01 — Flow

Three moves, and the third one is nothing

The partner does the regulated part. You keep the custody part. There is no point in the flow where a balance sits in someone else's ledger.

1

Name the amount

Enter what you want to spend and the wallet to receive to. The quote is locked for ninety seconds, fees shown before you commit, no hidden spread inside a fake rate.

Free · quote locked 90s
2

Pay the partner

Card or bank transfer to the licensed processor for your region. KYC is theirs — usually a document and a selfie, one time, then reused.

Card: seconds · Bank: 1–2h
3

It lands in your wallet

Straight to the address you gave. Nothing to claim, nothing to withdraw. Confirmation and receipt by email, hash on request.

03 — Terms worth reading

The parts that usually surprise people

Custody

Straight to you

The partner never holds a balance for you. If the destination address is wrong, it is gone — check it, the flow shows it back to you twice.

Rate

Quoted, then locked

Ninety seconds. Past that the quote re-prices rather than filling at a stale number.

Refunds

Failed card, auto-return

A declined or reversed card returns to source in 3–5 business days. No ticket needed.

Regions

22 restricted

Listed at §11. The partner refuses the payment before it is taken, not after.
